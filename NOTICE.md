# Attribution and port notes

BreakoutRadar-iOS is a native Swift/SwiftUI port based on the public Breakoutz Radar project by the Breakout Radar Contributors.

Original project: https://github.com/dezveda/breakoutz-radar
Original license: MIT

The iOS port rewrites the implementation in Swift and preserves the core scoring rules. It also restores a 24-hour volume prefilter for the Binance candidate universe and adds a user-selectable live-candle mode.
